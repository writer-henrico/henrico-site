I ran as fast as I could.

Leaves hit my face.

The forest grew denser as I ran.

I must’ve been running for an hour.

My legs wanted to give up.

But I kept moving forward.

The trees began to thin out, revealing a clearing bathed in moonlight.

I stopped.

That was the sign that my mother had told me about.

*If you get to that place, never go any further.*

I looked back one more time.

I didn’t know if they would catch up to me.

I thought of my aunt asking me to run.

*I have no choice.*

I turned my attention forward.

I kept running.

“Stop!”

A voice roared, echoing throughout the forest.

My muscles froze.

*Move!* I told myself.

But I was still frozen.

I looked around, searching for the source of the sound.

In the darkness, the tree trunks cast deceptive shadows that I mistook for silhouettes.

My mind was racing.

I was breathing heavily.

I looked everywhere.

There was movement in the bushes, slowly approaching me.

I didn’t feel threatened.

That was when I saw it.

Fangs, drool, and gray fur.

It was a wolf.

I let out a slight sigh of relief.

My shoulders relaxed for a moment.

He approached slowly and cautiously.

I saw dark marks in geometric shapes on his fur.

I took a fighting stance.

“Werewolf…”

He sniffed me.

He jumped back and started growling at me.

“You’re a vampire.”

I let out a soft laugh.

“And you’re a dog.”

He growled in protest.

“Insolent! Go right back where you came from.”

Slowly and instinctively, I reached for the knife in my boot.

Deep down, I already knew how this would end.

“I’d like to, but unfortunately, I can’t. Please, let me through.”

I felt the wooden handle of the small knife and gripped it without him noticing. Then I stood up, holding it in my hand.

“This forest is a sacred place. I can’t let a filthy creature like you cross it.”

“I’m sorry, but backing down isn’t an option for me. Let me through. I promise not to cause any trouble.”

“Never.”

Apparently, I had no choice.

“I’ll have to fight.”

The wolf crouched. I sensed his murderous intent.

I quickly sliced my left arm with the knife. The blood flowed out, then hardened into a thin, rigid sword.

The gray wolf leaped toward me.

I took a step to the left, dodging his fangs, and spun around to counterattack with my sword. He leaped backward, evading the blade.

He charged at me, trying to bite me from below.

I jumped backward to dodge his attack, but the wolf ducked beneath my sword.

Before I could land, he leaped at me.

I was still in midair. I couldn’t dodge it.

I grabbed his jaw with my left hand, preventing his fangs from reaching my face. His claws scratched my arm.

I saw an opening and slashed upward with my sword.

The wolf fell onto his back. I landed on the ground.

He got back up, growling.

“You’re hurt. I don’t want to kill you. Let me through.”

In response, he howled.

*This is bad.*

“Calling for backup?”

I smiled smugly.

“I thought we’d have some fun on our own.”

*Maybe I should back off. I can handle one of them, but several will be trouble.*

I slowly backed away.

Two more wolves appeared, one with black fur and the other with white fur. Both had the same pattern of symbols marked on their fur.

“Where’s the rest of the pack?” asked the wounded wolf.

“Many are still injured from yesterday’s attack,” replied the black wolf.

“And what’s going on?” asked the white female wolf.

“Another crazy vampire trying to get into our forest,” replied the wounded one.

“Another one?” I asked, confused. “Did she look like me?”

“Ye—”

“We don’t have to tell you anything,” the wounded wolf interrupted.

*Tsk.*

*Was it my mother? Why would she come here?*

I looked at the three creatures arguing among themselves. Sweat was dripping into my eyes.

I was breathless. It was getting hard to organize my thoughts.

“Why did that vampire come here?”

“We’re not going to tell you anything,” replied the wounded wolf.

*He won’t tell me, but I need to know. I might end up dying, but I think it’s worth it.*

“I’m sorry. I just realized that I can’t back down. I need to know the truth.”

I gripped the knife and cut my right arm.

Blood spilled out, but it didn’t fall to the ground. It floated around me while I controlled it.

That was my power.

The wolves retreated a bit.

*I can’t wait. If they surround me, I’ll lose.*

Adrenaline surged through me.

I charged at the wolf in the middle, using my blood to form thorns that attacked the other two while I struck the wounded creature.

All three reacted.

The wounded wolf bit my sword and held me in place, while the other two advanced from the flanks.

With a flick of my wrist, I released the blood thorns from the ground and launched them at the wolf holding me.

He let go of the sword.

I kicked the black wolf in the snout and tried to slash the white one, but she dodged.

The fight turned into a series of attacks and dodges. I managed to land blows on them more easily than I expected, but I also sustained some scratches.

With every wound, I lost more blood and grew weaker.

I jumped to avoid an attack, but when I landed, my supporting leg gave out. I lost my balance.

The three of them realized this and charged forward.

There was no way to dodge.

It was the end.

*At least I’d see my mother.*

I closed my eyes.

I felt myself flying away.

But I felt no pain.

The air smelled of aged whiskey.

I opened my eyes and saw a bearded man with slightly disheveled dark hair. A few strands fell across his forehead.

He was carrying me.

I closed my eyes again, exhausted.

I let myself be carried away.
