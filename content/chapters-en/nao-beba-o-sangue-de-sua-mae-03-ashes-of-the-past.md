I opened my eyes and saw a dim light falling on the wall of what appeared to be a wooden cabin.

I was lying in a bed covered with a thick, furry blanket.

*Where am I?* I thought, disoriented.

Memories began to return.

*I need to run! My family is going to catch me.*

I tried to get up quickly, but a wave of dizziness hit me, forcing me back onto the bed as if I were a piece of iron being pulled toward a magnet.

But I couldn’t give up.

Before I could try to get up again, a man’s voice spoke.

“Stay calm. You’re safe.”

I turned toward the sound and saw a figure sitting by the fireplace, tending the fire. I smelled something burning.

“Hanna, try not to push yourself. You’re injured.”

Once my eyes adjusted, I could make out the man. He had thick arms and wore an open dress shirt. His eyes were deep and distant.

I could sense the beast within him. That’s why I could tell he was a vampire.

“How do you know my name? Who are you?”

“I’m…” He paused for a second. “A friend of your mother’s.”

I’d never seen him before.

“And what’s your name?”

“Adrian.”

I really had no idea who he was.

He kept tending the fire.

I could see some charred paper.

My eyes were heavy.

I ended up closing my eyes and falling asleep.

I jolted awake and tried to get up again. This time, I managed to sit up without falling back onto the bed.

Adrian was sitting by the fireplace, holding an open book. The fire had died down, leaving only a few embers among the logs.

“You have to stop doing that,” Adrian warned, annoyed.

“How long did I sleep?”

“A few hours.”

“I need to go back to the forest. My mom went there.”

“You don’t need to go there. What your mom was looking for was something for her. You don’t need it.”

“But it’s the only clue I have about her. If she went there, I have to go.”

Adrian sighed and leaned forward in his chair.

“Young lady, you’re very stubborn. You really do take after your mother, both in personality and appearance. Especially with that hime cut.”

I smiled mischievously.

“You really notice the little things, huh? Do you like that hairstyle?”

He looked away.

“It’s none of your business.”

“You could’ve just said no, but it seems you do like my hair. Is there anything else you’d like to compliment me on?”

He fell silent.

“Did you like my mom? Were you close? Lovers, maybe?”

Adrian stopped turning the pages. He seemed about to answer, but instead, he slammed the book onto the table.

“Shut up!”

The shout startled me. I hadn’t expected him to react that way. I was speechless.

His eyes filled with tears.

He got up and walked toward what seemed to be the kitchen area of the cabin.

He returned holding a glass, looking much calmer.

“I’m sorry. I’m not ready to talk about your mother.”

“It’s okay. I went too far.”

He offered me the glass. The liquid looked like blood.

“You’ve lost a lot of blood. Are you thirsty?”

I looked at him suspiciously.

How could I trust blood from a stranger?

“How can I trust you? You’re a vampire, and you’ve never been to our family party.”

He chuckled softly.

“I could never go to one of your family’s parties.”

“Why not?”

He grew serious.

“Didn’t your mother ever tell you anything?”

“Tell me what?”

Adrian rolled his eyes.

“Just drink this blood. You’re going to need it.”

He offered me the glass again.

I noticed a mark on his hand. It looked like our clan’s mark, but the design was different.

I reached out with my right hand and took the glass.

“Thank you.”

My fingers touched his. Adrian didn’t let go of the glass right away, and our eyes met.

His eyes were beautiful.

I brought my free hand closer to his face. Adrian remained still for a moment. I felt his heart racing.

I leaned toward him.

Then his eyes widened. He grabbed my arm and pushed my hand away.

He turned his back to me and sighed softly.

I was annoyed.

*Why is he so resistant?*

I raised the glass to my lips and drank the blood.

My cuts were already healing, but that blood was different. It activated my powers and sped up my healing even more. I felt more powerful.

“This blood is special, isn’t it?”

“It’s a special kind of blood. It was hard to get.” Adrian smiled at me, but the smile quickly faded. “I was saving it for a special occasion… to drink with your mother.”

A question began to form in my mind.

“If you have no connection to my family, how do you know my mother died?”

I raised my voice in indignation.

“Do you know what happened to her? Did you see it happen? Why were you in the forest to save me?”

Adrian opened his mouth to answer, but a noise came from the doorway.

He stood up immediately and moved closer to me. He pulled a pocketknife from his pocket and sliced open his forearm. Blood dripped from the wound, stretching and twisting into a claw.

“Be quiet.”

Before I could ask anything, he covered my mouth with his free hand.

The door burst open.

Several well-dressed men entered the small cabin. It wasn’t even big enough for so many people.

“The rumors were true,” said one of the men who entered. “You really are a traitor. You’re hiding a woman from the Umbra clan.”

Adrian gritted his teeth.

“I’ll protect you, Hanna,” he whispered to me.

Adrian lunged at the nearest man and struck him with the blood claw, forcing him to retreat. When another tried to grab him from behind, Adrian spun around and struck him in the face with his elbow. While he was defending himself against the two of them, a third man struck him from behind.

Adrian staggered, giving the others the chance to knock him down. He tried to get up, but two men pinned him to the ground.

When they turned toward me, I raised my hands. I could barely stand, and fighting would only get me hurt too.

“I surrender.”

I was handcuffed and taken out of the cabin along with Adrian. Even with his hands tied, he kept trying to break free.

The men forced us to kneel side by side.

I looked at Adrian, but I didn’t know what to say.

I didn’t know what they would do to us.
